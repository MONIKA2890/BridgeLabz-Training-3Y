History of Paris

Paris is the capital city of France and has a long and fascinating history. It has played an important role in European art, culture, politics and education.

The city has developed over centuries and is now one of the most visited cities in the world.

Famous Places

Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is one of the most recognizable landmarks in the world.

Louvre Museum

The Louvre is one of the world's largest and most famous museums.

Parisian Culture

Paris is famous for its food, fashion, architecture, museums and artistic traditions.
